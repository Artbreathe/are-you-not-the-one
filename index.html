Save or share your list

Your list is saved on this device, in this browser only. It is private to you. Use Export now and then to keep a copy, for a physical journal or a backup.

To keep it on your phone: on Android, open the browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen. On Samsung phones, open this page in Chrome first. On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
